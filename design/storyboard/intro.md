# Intro storyboard (about 20 s, skippable at any time)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | A near-featureless whiteout. Wind-driven snow. "Visibility 30 m." A rover silhouette emerges as the fog thins. |
| 3 s | Headlights on. |
| 4.2 to 9.2 s | The camera tracks the rover across the ice as the light fades to dusk. "Ice under the tracks: N cm" (the real value of the cell). |
| 8.8 to 11.6 s | A crack forms under the camera and races outward along the route, glowing cyan. The camera shakes briefly. |
| 11.6 s | Instead of falling, the crack glows from within and turns Aurora Green: it is the route. The world goes to polar night; the aurora fills in. |
| 12.6 to 16.2 s | "Where the ice breaks, the route begins." The camera tilts up through the aurora. |
| 15.6 to 19 s | The camera climbs and turns to the top-down map; the route is the same line. The interface wipes in. |

Skip seeks to 15.6 s. Reduced motion shows the story as four static frames. Without WebGL the poster replaces the scene.
