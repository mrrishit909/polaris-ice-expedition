# Motion spec

- Routes etch in: a tube's draw range grows from the start over 1.4 s when a route is shown.
- The intro's crack is a window of the same tube around the camera's position that widens outward; its colour is the only change when it becomes the route.
- The storm front is a shader plane whose edge follows the domain's front function; scrubbing or playing the hour moves it, and the snow's intensity follows the storm at the centre.
- Aurora intensity follows the selected view very slightly (0.30 to 0.60), nothing more.
- Risky ice tilts, shrinks and separates; open water drops the chunk.
- Flying a route glides the camera behind and above the rover along the polyline over 9 s.
- Ambient motion (snow, aurora, storm texture, dish) stops under Pause Motion and reduced motion.
